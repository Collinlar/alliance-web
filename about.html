The story

Why Alliance exists

Kwame Odame spent years watching capable Ghanaian students get let down by agents who collected fees regardless of outcome. Copied SOPs. Poorly prepared financials. Wrong school choices. Avoidable refusals.

Alliance was built to be the opposite. A registered firm that tells you the truth about your profile and walks with you from first enquiry to safe arrival.

Mission

To open genuine global education pathways for African students through honest advice, accurate documentation and dependable support.

Vision

To become West Africa's most trusted educational brokerage network, connecting students and partner agents to opportunities worldwide.

Alliance told me the truth. When other agents gave me false hope, Alliance gave me a plan that actually worked.
