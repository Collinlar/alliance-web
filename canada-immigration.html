Straight answers

Before you book

Immigration decisions belong to the Canadian government. Our job is to give you honest advice and a complete application.

Is Alliance a regulated Canadian immigration consultancy?

No. Alliance Educational Consult is a Ghana-based education consultancy. Canadian immigration services are delivered in partnership with Purple Quartz Immigration Services and led by Tomi Orojinmi, a Regulated Canadian Immigration Consultant (RCIC-IRB). You can confirm her licence on the public register of the College of Immigration and Citizenship Consultants.

Can you guarantee my visa or permanent residence?

No one can. Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada makes every decision. We tell you honestly how strong your case is, prepare a complete application and correct weaknesses before you apply.

Can I meet the team in Accra?

Yes. Start with our Airport Residential Area office in Accra for your first conversation and document review. Consultations with Tomi are arranged through the Alliance team.

I am already studying with Alliance. Do I start again?

No. Your admission file already tells us a lot. We continue from there, so your study permit, work permit and later permanent residence plan stay connected.

What should I bring to the first consultation?

Your passport, your latest resume, your education certificates and any past visa or travel history. If you have a school offer or a job offer, bring that too. If you were refused before, bring the refusal letter.
