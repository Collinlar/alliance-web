# Homepage photo slots

Drop these three files in this folder. The homepage detects them automatically,
tints each one with its bar colour, and hides the drawn placeholder scene.
Until a file exists, the bar shows its colour treatment and scene only.

| File | Bar | What to supply |
|---|---|---|
| `study.webp`  | 01 STUDY  | Ghanaian or African student, mid-shot, on a university campus (library, quad, lecture hall). Looking up or slightly off camera, hopeful. Holding books or a laptop. Teal tint is added in CSS, so a bright, natural-colour photo works best. |
| `move.webp`   | 02 MOVE   | African professional or student in a contemporary Canadian setting (modern campus, office lobby, transit hub, glass buildings). No flags, no CN Tower or postcard skyline. Cool daylight or dusk. Navy tint is added in CSS. |
| `settle.webp` | 03 SETTLE | African professional, couple or young family in a residential or park setting that feels like a new home (autumn light is fine). Warm, golden-hour light. Charcoal and gold tint is added in CSS. |

Specs
- Portrait or square crop, subject in the upper 60% (the bottom 40% sits under the text).
- Leave the left third of the frame calm; the title sits bottom-left.
- 1600 px on the long edge, WebP (or JPG), under 250 KB each (compress with Squoosh or TinyPNG).
- Use licensed or commissioned images only. Model release needed for every recognisable face.
- Tomi Orojinmi's portrait is already in `tomi-orojinmi.webp`. Confirm with Purple Quartz that Alliance may use it.
